What is Hydraulic Oil and Its Role in Your Machinery

Hydraulic oil is the fluid that transmits power inside hydraulic systems — presses, lifts, injection moulding machines, cranes, and countless other machines that rely on fluid pressure to move heavy loads. Beyond just transmitting force, this oil also lubricates moving parts, carries away heat, and protects internal components from rust and wear.

A hydraulic system is only as reliable as the oil running through it — poor-quality oil can lead to valve sticking, foaming, overheating, and eventually costly equipment failure. This is why choosing the right hydraulic oil manufacturer is a decision that directly affects machine uptime and maintenance costs.
