Features That Matter on a Precision Shop Floor

The Cut-S and Cut-S Super formulations mix evenly with water and hold that mix without separating quickly, which matters for machines running long, unattended shifts. A low-foam formula keeps coolant delivery steady at high pump pressure, and good filterability means the fluid doesn't clog fine machine filters — a real issue on close-tolerance CNC work.

The straight oils in the range carry strong anti-rust and extreme-pressure additives, protecting both the tool edge and the finished component from corrosion during and after machining.
