What Does Bike Engine Oil Actually Do?

A motorcycle engine works far harder than most people realize. It runs at high RPMs, deals with constant heat, and in most Indian bikes, the same oil also lubricates the clutch and gearbox. Bike engine oil reduces friction between moving engine parts, keeps the engine cool, prevents sludge and carbon build-up, protects against corrosion, and ensures smooth gear shifting since the clutch runs in the same oil.

This is why choosing the right bike engine oil manufacturer is not something to take lightly — a poor-quality oil can lead to engine wear, rough gear shifts, and costly repairs over time.
