What Is Cutting Oil and Why It Matters for Kerala's Industries

Cutting oil, also called cutting fluid, is used during machining operations such as turning, drilling, milling, and grinding. When a cutting tool works on metal at high speed, it generates heat and friction. Without proper lubrication, this heat damages the tool, reduces surface finish quality, and slows down production.

For industries in Kerala working with rubber processing equipment, automotive components, marine engineering parts, and general fabrication, a good cutting oil reduces friction, controls heat, protects tools and workpieces from rust, and keeps machining consistent. This is exactly why choosing a genuine cutting oil manufacturer matters — a poor-quality oil raises tool replacement costs and slows down every job that depends on it.
